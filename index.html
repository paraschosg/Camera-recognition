Gesture Singer

Move your hands to sing. The camera also reads your mood, the room light and what is going on in the scene.

Press Start to allow camera and audio.
How to sing: raise a hand to go higher, lower it to go deeper. Open your hand for loud, close it for silence. Slide left/right to change the vowel (oo · oh · ah · eh · ee). Pinch thumb and index for vibrato. Use both hands for a duet.
