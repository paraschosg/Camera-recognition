The camera recognises your hand gestures, reads your mood, measures the room light and describes what is going on in the scene.

Press Start to allow the camera.
Try it: show an open palm, a fist, a pinch, a pointing finger, a peace sign or a thumbs up. Smile, frown or look surprised. Dim the lights.

How to set up the bridge
  1. Install Python 3, then in the project folder run pip install -r bridge/requirements.txt.
  2. Run python3 bridge/bridge.py and keep that window open.
  3. Choose a mode above. The status turns green when connected.

Hand pointer: index finger moves the cursor, pinch thumb + index to click or drag, thumb + middle finger right-clicks, peace sign + move up/down scrolls, fist rests the cursor, hold an open palm to pause / resume.
Head pointer: move your head to move the cursor, open your mouth to click, raise your eyebrows to right-click, tilt your head to scroll.
